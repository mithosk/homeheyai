## Creation & History

Created by writer **Stan Lee** and artist **Jack Kirby**, the Hulk made his debut in Marvel Comics' *The Incredible Hulk #1* in May 1962. Lee drew inspiration from a combination of classic literature, including Mary Shelley's *Frankenstein* and Robert Louis Stevenson's *Strange Case of Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde*, alongside cold-war nuclear anxiety.

In his initial appearance, the Hulk was actually **grey**. However, due to color printing inconsistencies at the printer, Lee and Kirby switched his skin color to **green** starting in issue #2—a choice that permanently defined the character's signature look.

## Origin Story

**Dr. Bruce Banner** was a brilliant, introverted nuclear physicist working for the U.S. Armed Forces on a experimental bomb project powered by **Gamma Radiation** at a test site in New Mexico.

During the maiden test detonation of the Gamma Bomb, teenager **Rick Jones** inadvertently trespassed onto the bombing field. Banner rushed out to save the youth, shoving Jones into a protective trench just as the bomb detonated. Banner was absorbed by an overwhelming dose of gamma radiation.

Although expected to die, Banner survived. However, the extreme exposure mutated his DNA, unlocking a terrifying inner monster. Initially transforming at nightfall (and later triggered by intense anger and stress), Banner transforms into a towering, green-skinned behemoth of destruction known as **The Hulk**.

## Powers, Abilities & Transformations

### Superhuman Powers
* **Infinite Physical Strength**: The Hulk's strength is directly proportional to his emotional state—the angrier he gets, the stronger he becomes ("*The madder Hulk gets, the stronger Hulk gets!*").
* **Near-Limitless Stamina & Invulnerability**: Immune to conventional gunfire, artillery, extreme atmospheric pressures, and environmental hazards.
* **Accelerated Healing Factor**: Regenerates damaged tissue and organ systems at a rate surpassing almost any other hero in the Marvel Universe.
* **Superhuman Leaping**: Able to leap thousands of miles across continents in a single bound due to massive leg muscle density.
* **Gamma Energy Emission**: Can release catastrophic shockwaves via clapping his hands ("Thunderclap") or emitting raw radioactive energy.

### Major Incarnations & Alters
Due to Bruce Banner's childhood trauma and Dissociative Identity Disorder (DID), the Hulk manifests in several distinct personalities:

* **Savage Hulk**: The most recognizable version—childlike, emotionally driven, immensely powerful, and seeking peace.
* **Joe Fixit (Grey Hulk)**: Cunning, street-smart, Vegas-bouncertype persona who trades raw force for strategy and pragmatism.
* **Professor / Merged Hulk**: A fusion of Banner's intellect with the Hulk's physical prowess and confidence.
* **World-Breaker Hulk**: The most devastating, radiation-emitting form achieved after extreme rage and tragedy (*Planet Hulk / World War Hulk*).
* **Immortal Hulk**: A horror-focused, nocturnal entity incapable of truly dying, driven by cosmic-gamma forces.

## Core Themes & Dual Persona

1. **Jekyll and Hyde Dynamic**: Bruce Banner is a peaceful genius who fears his own inner rage, while the Hulk is an engine of destruction seeking isolation from a world that fears him.
2. **Childhood Trauma**: Modern lore reveals Banner's mental fracturing originated long before the Gamma Bomb, stemming from severe abuse by his father, Brian Banner.
3. **Nuclear Anxiety**: The Hulk serves as a living metaphor for the uncontrolled power and collateral damage of nuclear warfare.

## The Rogues' Gallery

* **The Abomination (Emil Blonsky)**: A former Soviet spy intentionally exposed to a higher dose of gamma radiation, retaining his mind but permanently trapped in a reptilian-like monster form.
* **The Leader (Samuel Sterns)**: A worker exposed to gamma radiation who gained superhuman intelligence and an oversized cranium instead of physical strength, serving as Banner's intellectual arch-enemy.
* **Red Hulk (Thaddeus Ross)**: General Ross transformed via tactical gamma rays into a heat-generating counterpart to destroy the original Hulk.
* **Maestro**: An alternate future version of the Hulk who possesses Banner's intellect and a ruthless desire to conquer a post-apocalyptic Earth.

## Iconic Comic Book Storylines

1. **"Planet Hulk"** (*2006–2007*)
   * Exiled into deep space by the Illuminati, the Hulk lands on the gladiatorial planet Sakaar, leads a revolution, and becomes its ruler.
2. **"World War Hulk"** (*2007*)
   * Fueled by vengeance after a tragedy on Sakaar, the Hulk returns to Earth to single-handedly defeat the Illuminati and the Earth's heroes.
3. **"Future Imperfect"** (*1992–1993*)
   * Banner travels to a dystopian future where he confronts his tyrannical future self, Maestro.
4. **"The Immortal Hulk"** (*2018–2021, by Al Ewing*)
   * A critically acclaimed cosmic-horror run redefining the gamma lore, body horror, and Banner's immortality.
