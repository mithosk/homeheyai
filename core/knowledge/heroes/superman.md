## Origin Story

Born on the dying planet **Krypton**, infant **Kal-El** was rocketed to Earth by his scientist father, Jor-El, moments before Krypton's destruction. 

His pod crash-landed in the rural town of **Smallville, Kansas**, where he was found and adopted by **Jonathan and Martha Kent**. Raised as **Clark Kent**, he was taught strong moral values alongside managing his extraordinary abilities, powered by Earth's yellow sun. 

As an adult, Clark moved to the bustling city of **Metropolis**, working as an investigative reporter for *The Daily Planet* while secretly defending humanity as **Superman**.

---

## Powers and Abilities

* **Superhuman Strength:** Capable of moving planets, lifting immense weight, and overwhelming physical forces.
* **Flight:** Can navigate seamlessly through Earth's atmosphere and deep space at speeds exceeding light.
* **Invulnerability:** Virtually immune to conventional physical trauma, extreme temperatures, and radiation.
* **Heat Vision:** Emits concentrated beams of intense thermal energy from his eyes.
* **Freeze Breath:** Can freeze objects and opponents by exhaling high-velocity cold air.
* **Enhanced Senses:** Possesses X-ray vision, telescopic vision, microscopic vision, and super-hearing across vast distances.
* **Superhuman Speed & Stamina:** Able to move fast enough to match speedsters and battle for extended periods without fatigue.

---

## Key Weaknesses

> While near-godlike under a yellow sun, Superman remains vulnerable to specific forces:

* **Kryptonite:** Irradiated fragments of his destroyed home planet. 
  * **Green Kryptonite:** Weakens him and causes severe physical pain.
  * **Red Kryptonite:** Causes unpredictable physiological and psychological effects.
* **Magic:** Mystical energy bypasses his biologicalinvulnerability.
* **Red Sun Radiation:** Deprives him of his stored solar powers, rendering him as vulnerable as a normal human.

## Major Allies & Enemies

### Supporting Cast & Allies
* **Lois Lane:** Pulitzer Prize-winning journalist and Clark's primary romantic interest/wife.
* **Jimmy Olsen:** Photographer for *The Daily Planet* and Clark's close friend.
* **Batman (Bruce Wayne):** Fellow superhero and strategic counterpart in the Justice League.
* **Wonder Woman (Diana Prince):** Amazonian warrior and trusted comrade.

### Arch-Nemeses
* **Lex Luthor:** A ruthless billionaire industrialist who views Superman as an existential threat to humanity.
* **Brainiac:** An alien collector of worlds who shrinks and bottles alien civilizations.
* **General Zod:** A military leader from Krypton who seeks to rebuild his lost home at Earth's expense.
* **Doomsday:** A genetically engineered force of destruction famous for killing Superman in battle.
